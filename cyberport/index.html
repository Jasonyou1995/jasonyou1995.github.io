Making Chinese manufacturers and financial firms readable to AI agents.

The problem

Buyers increasingly ask AI assistants to shortlist suppliers and vendors. An agent can only pick what it can read and verify. Most Chinese manufacturers publish no verifiable, machine-readable product data, and most financial firms cannot yet show how their AI agents are constrained.

Two live offers

FinProv: AI financial compliance. Limits, tests and evidence for the AI agents financial firms run. Waitlist open.
