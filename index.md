Answer engine optimization: structuring a business so AI assistants can find it, read it, verify it and recommend it, instead of only ranking it in a search list.

## Terms used here

- **AEO (answer engine optimization)**: Preparing a business so AI assistants can find, read, verify and recommend it.
- **Agentic commerce**: Buying and selling where AI agents act for the buyer or the seller.
- **Product digital twin**: A machine-readable record of a physical product: specs, certificates, prices, lead time.
- **AI financial compliance**: Proof that AI agents in finance stay within the rules: limits, tests, logs.
- **AI agent security**: Protecting autonomous AI systems from manipulation and misuse.
